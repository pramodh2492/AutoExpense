AutoExpense automatically reads your bank SMS and tracks every rupee you spend — so you don't have to.

💡 HOW IT WORKS
Just install and grant SMS permission. AutoExpense reads transaction messages from your bank (Axis, HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Kotak, and 20+ banks), extracts the amount, merchant, and date — and categorizes it automatically. No manual entry needed.

SMS PERMISSION DECLARATION (for Play Store review)
================================================================
Core Functionality: AutoExpense is a personal finance tracker that reads
bank transaction SMS messages to automatically log and categorize expenses.

Why READ_SMS is needed: The app's primary feature is automatic expense
tracking from bank SMS. Without READ_SMS, users would need to manually
enter every transaction, defeating the app's core purpose.

Why RECEIVE_SMS is needed: To detect new bank transaction messages in
real-time and immediately log them as expenses, providing instant
notifications and up-to-date spending data.

What we read: ONLY messages from known bank sender IDs (AXISBK, HDFCBK,
SBIINB, ICICIB, KOTAKB, CUBANK, etc.) that contain transaction keywords
(debited, credited, spent, paid, etc.)

What we DO NOT read: Personal messages, OTPs, promotional SMS, or any
non-banking messages.

Data handling: All SMS parsing happens entirely on-device. Raw message
content is never transmitted to external servers.
